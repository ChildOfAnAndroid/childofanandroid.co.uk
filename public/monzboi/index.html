monzboi

Monzboi is a personal experimental automation project and testing surface for ICHARIS2.

It is used by its owner to test email workflow, queueing, provenance, failure recovery, and human-in-the-loop automation against the owner's own accounts and data.

Monzboi is not a public banking product, is not offered for general customer use, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Monzo Bank Limited.

privacy terms childofanandroid.co.uk

what it does

Monzboi experiments with safely finding relevant messages, maintaining ordered queues, creating threaded Gmail drafts, sending configured replies, and recording enough state to recover from interrupted runs without duplicating work.

It is essentially an experimental proving ground for parts of ICHARIS2, a broader personal information and automation system.

google account access

When authorised by its owner, Monzboi may use Gmail permissions to inspect message metadata needed for threading and identity checks, and to create or send Gmail drafts and messages as part of the configured workflow.

The current Google OAuth integration requests Gmail compose and metadata access. The app is intended for the owner's own Google account, not for collecting data from members of the public.
