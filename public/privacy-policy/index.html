monzboi // privacy

privacy

This notice describes how Monzboi handles information when used by its owner as a personal experimental automation tool and ICHARIS2 testing surface.

monzboi terms childofanandroid.co.uk

data accessed

When the owner connects a Google account, Monzboi may access Gmail metadata required to identify messages and conversations, and may create, update, or send drafts and messages through Gmail when the configured workflow requires it.

The current Google OAuth integration requests the gmail.compose and gmail.metadata scopes. Other local integrations may process the owner's own email data on the owner's device where separately configured.

why it is used

Google account data is used only to operate and test the owner's configured email automation, including message identification, thread integrity checks, draft creation, sending, duplicate prevention, and recovery after interrupted runs.

storage & retention

Operational state, credentials, and queue records are intended to remain under the owner's control, principally on the owner's own systems. Gmail messages and drafts remain stored by Google in the connected account.

Monzboi does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to data brokers. It is not designed to build advertising profiles or to offer Gmail data to third parties.

sharing

Information is sent to Google where necessary to use Gmail services. Monzboi may also interact with the intended recipients of messages the owner has configured it to draft or send. No separate public user-data marketplace or advertising disclosure is part of the project.

control & deletion

The owner can revoke Monzboi's Google access from their Google Account permissions. Local credentials and Monzboi runtime state can also be deleted from the owner's systems. Revoking access prevents further Gmail API access until the owner authorises it again.
